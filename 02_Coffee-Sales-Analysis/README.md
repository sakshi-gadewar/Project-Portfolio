### Coffee Sales Analysis - Excel Dashboard

An interactive Coffee Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze coffee shop sales performance, revenue, profit, transaction trends, product categories, cities, and time-based sales patterns.

The dashboard transforms raw coffee sales data into an interactive business report that helps identify top-performing categories, revenue trends, profitable periods, and city-wise performance.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel and creating an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of:

- Sales and revenue performance
- Transaction volume
- Product category performance
- Monthly transaction trends
- Weekday transaction patterns
- Yearly revenue contribution
- City-wise revenue performance
- Monthly revenue and profit
- Category-wise revenue

The dashboard allows users to interact with the data using Year, Month, and City filters to explore sales performance from different perspectives.
  
---

## Business Problem Statement

Coffee businesses generate large amounts of transaction data across different products, cities, and time periods. Analyzing raw transaction data manually can make it difficult to identify sales trends and understand business performance.

This dashboard was created to answer questions such as:

- What is the total revenue and profit generated?
- Which coffee categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which cities contribute the most to revenue?
- Which months have the highest transaction volume?
- Which weekdays generate the most transactions?
- How does revenue change across different years?
- Which product categories contribute the most to overall sales?
- How can Excel dashboards help stakeholders monitor business performance?

  ## Key Insights

- The business generated 9,971 transactions with a total quantity sold of 14,117.
  Total revenue was approximately $46.37K.
- Total profit was approximately $16.23K.
- Desserts generated the highest revenue among the product categories shown in the dashboard.
- Revenue was distributed across multiple cities, with Chicago and Los Angeles among the stronger contributors.
- Friday recorded the highest transaction count among the weekdays shown.
- The monthly transaction analysis shows fluctuations in customer purchasing activity throughout the year.
- Yearly revenue contribution can be compared using the interactive year filter and yearly revenue visualization.
